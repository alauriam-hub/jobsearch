Executive Profile
Angela Kingdon is the person you bring in when a campaign needs to move from idea to execution without losing momentum along the way. She has built and run content ecosystems across podcasts, newsletters, and social platforms, reaching over 1M downloads and 10k+ subscribers. What that reflects is not just audience size, but an ability to understand how attention works, how distribution happens, and how to turn strategy into something that actually lands.
Her work sits at the intersection of creators, clients, and content. She identifies the right talent, aligns them with clear objectives, and manages campaigns from concept through delivery. She is comfortable handling multiple projects at once, managing timelines, budgets, and expectations while maintaining a consistent standard of quality. She focuses on what matters once the campaign goes live. Engagement, response, and performance against agreed KPIs. She uses that data to refine and improve, ensuring each campaign performs better than the last.
Known for her calm, structured approach, she builds trust quickly with both clients and teams. She communicates clearly, resolves issues early, and takes full ownership of delivery. The result is work that is not only creative, but reliable, repeatable, and effective.
