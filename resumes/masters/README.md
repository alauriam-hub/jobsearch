# Master Résumés — drop zone

**Add your master résumé(s) here.** Any format works: `.pdf`, `.docx`, `.md`, or a plain-text paste in a new `.md` file.

If you maintain several (e.g. one leaning *executive marketing/CMO*, one leaning *AI evaluation/research*, one leaning *content/communications*), add them all and name them clearly, e.g.:
- `master-executive-marketing.md`
- `master-ai-evaluation.md`
- `master-communications-content.md`

The agent will, per opportunity: pick the closest master → rewrite the executive summary to the job → reorder and surface relevant accomplishments → cut the irrelevant → ATS-optimise keywords → quantify → keep to 1–2 pages (more only for executive roles). It will **never** fabricate or exaggerate beyond what your masters state.

Until at least one master is here, the agent can score and recommend jobs and draft cover letters from your profile, but cannot generate tailored résumés.
